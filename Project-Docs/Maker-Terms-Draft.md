## Page 1 — Maker terms

**Title:** Maker terms, in plain English

**Your site and your work are yours.**
Your photos, your words and your products belong to you. You give BohdiAI permission to show them on your site and to use them to build it, and nothing else. When you leave, they go with you (see Leaving below).

**You're the shop.**
BohdiAI makes and runs your website. You're the one selling. Your products, your prices, your shipping, your returns, your customers and your taxes are yours to look after. If a customer has a problem with an order, they come to you.

**BohdiAI never takes a cut.**
When a customer pays you, the money goes straight to your own Square or Stripe. BohdiAI never touches it and never takes a percentage. You pay BohdiAI your plan, and that's all.

**Your plan.**
Your plan is billed by Stripe, monthly or yearly, until you change it. Moving up happens straight away and you pay the difference for the rest of your term. Moving down happens at your next renewal. There are no refunds for part of a term.

**Leaving.**
You can cancel any time from Your plan. Your site moves to Free at the end of your term and goes offline until you pick a plan again. If you'd rather leave completely, Close my account removes your site and everything in it for good at the end of your term.

**What can't go on a site.**
Some things can't be sold or shown on any BohdiAI site, listed on the page "What can't be sold or shown". When a site is built, and when something new is saved, it's checked against that list. Anything that looks like it breaks it is held until Alex has a look.

**When a site comes down.**
If a site breaks that list, or is used to cheat people, Alex can take it offline. You'll get an email saying why, and you can reply to talk about it. A site that keeps breaking it, or does something seriously harmful, can be closed.

**Keeping your account safe.**
Keep your password to yourself. You're responsible for what's done in your account. If you think someone else has got in, tell Alex straight away.

**What BohdiAI promises, and doesn't.**
BohdiAI works hard to keep your site up and your information safe, but can't promise it will never go down or never have a problem. BohdiAI isn't responsible for lost sales or other losses from your site being offline, from a mistake on it, or from a customer, Square or Stripe. If something goes wrong, the most BohdiAI owes you is what you paid for your plan in the last three months.

**Changes to these terms.**
If these terms change, you'll get an email before the new ones start. Keeping your site after that means you agree to them.

**Governing law.** (added to match the visitor terms)
These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Rhode Island, USA. Any dispute that can't be sorted out by email goes to the state or federal courts in Rhode Island.

## Page 2 — What can't be sold or shown

**Title:** What can't be sold or shown

**Intro:** BohdiAI is for people who make things. To keep it a good place for makers and their customers, none of these can be sold or shown on a BohdiAI site:

- Weapons, ammunition, and parts for them
- Drugs, vapes, and anything sold as medicine or as a cure
- Adult sexual content
- Hate, threats or harassment of anyone
- Anything stolen, fake or counterfeit, including fake brand names and someone else's characters or logos
- Other people's photos, art or designs passed off as your own
- Live animals
- Scams: fake giveaways, or asking shoppers to pay somewhere other than your site's checkout or the payment options you list
- Anyone's private details, such as addresses or phone numbers, without their say-so

**Closing:** Not sure about something you make? Email Alex at {email} before you add it. Seen a BohdiAI site breaking this list? Use "Report this site" at the bottom of that site.
