# Maker-Specific Apps for Craft Shows, Markets, Profitability, Sales and Inventory (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-10-13. US focus. Search used about 22 tool calls; several sites (boothtally.com, boothkeeper.com, thecraftmap.com, Etsy listing pages) returned 403/429 or empty pages, so some facts come from search-result snippets only. These are flagged.

## Craft business apps (Craftybase/Stocksmith, Craftmaker Pro, Artwork Archive, Inventora, CraftsTrack): which track shows and per-show profit?

### Takeaway
The established maker back-office apps cover inventory, cost of goods and pricing. They treat a craft fair as one more sales channel, usually through a Square sync, and none I found keeps a per-market record (fee, organizer, notes, review) or reports profit per show. They are also priced well above $15-25 once a seller grows (Craftybase/Stocksmith runs $20 to $349/mo).

### Cited Findings
- Craftybase has rebranded as **Stocksmith**. Its pricing page says "Craftybase → Stocksmith · Same team, same software — just a new name." — [Stocksmith pricing](https://stocksmith.io/pricing/)
- Stocksmith tiers: Pro "from $20/mo" (one sales channel); Studio ~$41/mo annual or $49/mo monthly (my read of unlabeled prices); Indie $83/mo annual or $99/mo monthly (1,000 order lines/mo); Business $166-199/mo (up to 5,000); Growth $291-349/mo. The pricing page does not mention craft shows, markets, POS or Square. — [Stocksmith pricing](https://stocksmith.io/pricing/)
- Craftybase marketing says it tracks stock across Etsy, Shopify and in-person craft fairs, with in-person sales coming through its Square integration so that one inventory count covers both market and online sales. — [Craftybase craft inventory software](https://app.craftybase.com/craft-inventory-software); [Craftybase Etsy integration](https://craftybase.com/integrations/etsy)
- Third-party Craftybase prices conflict: Capterra lists "starting $99/month" and AlternativeTo lists $19-59/mo. Both are probably stale against the Stocksmith page. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/compare/135618-144117/Odoo-vs-Craftybase); [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/craftybase/about)
- Craftybase gives away a free printable craft-show inventory sheet to tape to the stall table, which is a content-marketing nod to market sellers. — [Craftybase blog](https://craftybase.com/blog/inventory-management/free-craft-show-inventory-template)
- **Inventora** (Shopify app) costs from $23/month with a free trial. It is raw-material and COGS inventory for makers. I found nothing on markets or in-person sales. — [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/inventora)
- **Artwork Archive** is an artist/collector inventory with sales tracking, invoicing and PayPal. Third-party price listings conflict: artist plans from about $6-9/mo up to a $36-42/mo "Master" plan, and organization plans $24-59/mo. Nothing found is specific to art-fair or booth workflows. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/169087/Artwork-Archive/); [FitGap](https://us.fitgap.com/products/artwork-archive)
- **CraftsTrack** (2026, priced in Singapore dollars) offers project costing, materials, pricing, "quick sales" logged "at a market, on Etsy, or through custom orders," invoicing, and P&L/cash-flow/tax reports. It has a free tier (5 products, 30 days of history) and Pro at S$299.99/yr (~S$25/mo). No event tracking and no per-event profit. — [CraftsTrack](https://craftstrack.app/)
- **Craftmaker Pro**: I found no current pricing or feature page. Only a Craftybase comparison page turned up. — [Craftybase compare](https://craftybase.com/compare/craft-maker-pro)

### Inferences
- The incumbents are inventory/COGS tools. Their nearest market feature is "Square sales reduce stock." The per-market ledger the BohdiAI app offers (fees, organizer, review, take-home stock, sales minus costs) is not their focus.
- Stocksmith's move up-market (top tier $349/mo) leaves a low-price gap for hobby and part-time makers.

### Gaps
- Could not verify Craftmaker Pro, Maker's Ledger, Marmalead or Etsy's own seller tools for event features. No primary pages were found in this pass. Marmalead is an Etsy SEO tool, so market tracking is unlikely, but that is unverified.
- No user counts were found for any of these apps.

## Craft show / vendor tracking apps: what they do, cost, and who uses them

### Takeaway
2025-2026 brought a wave of small, new, indie vendor-tracking apps: BoothBrain, VendStats, BoothTally, BoothKeeper, Before The Booth, MyEventPrep, Vendor Toolkit/FairKeep and The Craft Map. Almost all are under a year old with only a handful of App Store ratings. That shows the need is real but no winner yet exists. BoothBrain comes closest to the BohdiAI booth piece: it is a phone POS that records cash and links out to Square, Venmo, Cash App and PayPal, with revenue by payment method. Market organizer platforms (Marketspread) serve vendors only as a side line.

### Cited Findings
**Vendor-side apps (closest competitors)**
- **BoothBrain** (iOS, Steven Song / Everup Digital LLC): inventory with SKUs and low-stock alerts; a POS calculator with discounts, automatic sales tax, cash recording and voids; event planning with prep checklists and pre-staged inventory; sales reports with "revenue by payment method"; team sharing by join code. It does not process payments. It opens Square, Venmo, Cash App and PayPal. IAPs: BoothBrain Pro $9.99/mo and Power Vendor $14.99/mo. v1.0 released 12/29/2025. Rated 5.0 from **1 rating**. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/boothbrain/id6754667277)
- **VendStats: Sales Tracker** (iPhone/iPad, CODEWERX LLC): quick sale logging, item costs, expenses, margins, net profit, inventory with low-stock alerts, best sellers, event-to-event comparison, CSV export. Pro is $4.99/mo or $29.99/yr. Rated 5.0 from **3 ratings**. Versions run from 1.0 (Feb 26) to 2.0.2 (Jul 7), which is likely 2026 given the app ID range. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vendstats-sales-tracker/id6759027702)
- **BoothTally** (web, offline): **$6.99 one-time**, no account. Logs booth fees, sales and expenses per fair. Its dashboard gives a verdict per event: Rebook (cleared $150 net), Watch fees (profitable but thin), or Skip (lost money after fees and travel). The site returned 403, so this comes from search snippets only. — [BoothTally](https://boothtally.com/)
- **BoothKeeper**: a free profit tracker built around a minimum acceptable hourly rate per show. Its example: $1,200 in sales, 20 hours and $400 fixed costs works out to about $40/hr and $800 net. From search snippets; the page body was empty. — [BoothKeeper](https://www.boothkeeper.com/)
- **Before The Booth** (web): festival application pipeline, deadline alerts, fee logging, color-coded season calendar, map, expense tracking per festival, ROI by event, booth layout planner, mileage and hotel tracking, contact book, CSV. Free tier (unlimited festivals, applications, expenses); **Pro $12/mo** (directory, financial dashboard, revenue tracking, mileage); **Pro Plus $18/mo** (a food-vendor tier: inventory, menus, permits). The directory counters show placeholder "0K+", which suggests it is very early. — [Before The Booth](https://www.beforethebooth.com/)
- **MyEventPrep** (web, early access, free "while we grow"): event planning, production planning from an imported Square catalog, a weather-aware packing list, event recaps (sales, sell-through, reflections), a Square sales import matched to each event, and "vendor neighbor notes." It works offline. It says outright it is not a POS and not full inventory or accounting. — [MyEventPrep](https://myeventprep.app/)
- **Vendor Toolkit** (VARØ Industries, early access, browser): free calculators (sales tax, pricing, a card-fee reconciler covering Square, Stripe, PayPal, Etsy and Venmo Business, break-even, cash change, packing list, inventory counter, QR code generator, price tags). **FairFinder** is a free fair search. **FairKeep Pro** (account, price not shown) logs income, expenses, mileage and booth details, reports profit per hour, and syncs with Shopify, Etsy, Google Calendar and QuickBooks. No POS, no website. — [Vendor Toolkit](https://vendortools.app/)
- **The Craft Map**: a craft fair directory with a paid vendor tier at about **$15/month** that includes expense and sales tracking and a vendor profit/ROI dashboard. It cites Craftybase at ~$19/mo as an inventory option. This comes from search snippets; the pricing page returned 429. — [The Craft Map pricing](https://www.thecraftmap.com/pricing); [The Craft Map blog](https://www.thecraftmap.com/blog/craft-fair-roi-tracking-profit)

**Organizer platforms with a vendor side**
- **Marketspread** is mainly market-manager software (applications, vendor fees by type, day, category or sales percentage). Vendors use it free, with optional paid upgrades. Vendor add-ons: a **POS app at $18/year** plus card processing (iOS/Android, swipe/insert/tap hardware), an online store with a free custom web address and inventory synced with in-person sales, pickup/delivery/shipping, customer capture, email/SMS, and "Automatic Market Sync" that adds pickup windows for booked markets. The online store costs **2.5% per order** (Growth) or **$129/mo flat**. — [Marketspread vendor sales](https://marketspread.com/online-sales-for-small-vendors); [Marketspread fees](https://marketspread.com/learning/articles/day-of-week-vendor-fees/); [Food Trust vendor page](https://thefoodtrust.org/?p=209)

**Show application platforms**
- **ZAPP (Zapplication)** and **EntryThingy** are juried art-show application platforms that artists submit to. Some EntryThingy listings redirect to ZAPP. ZAPP's 2024 year-in-review: average jury fee **$34.70**, average booth fee **$439.58**. Artists on Art Fair Insiders complain about $30+ minimum jury fees. These do not track sales or profit. — [EntryThingy](https://app.entrythingy.com/calls/external/zapp-training-show-2025/); [ZAPP blog](https://blog.zapplication.org/?p=4320); [Art Fair Insiders](https://www.artfairinsiders.com/forum/topics/question-for-artists-applying-to-shows)

### Inferences
- The space is crowded with micro-launches, nearly all from late 2025 into 2026, with one to three ratings each. Nobody has traction yet, so there is no dominant brand to displace, but there are many cheap options ($0, $6.99 once, $4.99/mo).
- BoothBrain's model of recording Venmo, Cash App and cash without processing payments matches the BohdiAI POS approach almost exactly, at $9.99-14.99/mo for the POS alone. That makes $15/mo for site + catalog + POS + market records look strong on value.
- The "is this show worth repeating" verdict (BoothTally) and per-hour profit (BoothKeeper, FairKeep) are recurring ideas. Several builders saw the same need.
- None of these apps offers a buyer-facing QR self-checkout page tied to the market day, which is part of the BohdiAI offer.

### Gaps
- Could not find live products named "Craft Show Tracker," "Vendor Tracker," "Booth Buddy," "Sale Tracker," "Pop Up Shop," "Market Mate," "Manage My Market," "MarketWurks," "Farmspread" or "Craftlink" in this pass. From general knowledge, Manage My Market and MarketWurks are organizer-side market management tools, but that is unverified here, so treat it as unconfirmed.
- No user counts, Google Play ratings or download figures for any vendor app.
- No Reddit threads surfaced in search (results returned Etsy forums and blogs instead), so direct user sentiment about these apps is missing.
- BoothTally, BoothKeeper and Craft Map details come from search snippets, not full page reads.

## Any all-in-one product combining maker website + booth POS + market tracking?

### Takeaway
No maker-focused product found combines all three. **Marketspread** comes closest on website + POS (an online store plus a $18/yr POS app with market pickup sync), but it is built for markets, not makers. It has no per-market profit or review record, and its store costs 2.5% per order or $129/mo, which conflicts with a no-cut-of-sales stance. The other options each cover one or two pieces.

### Cited Findings
- Marketspread vendor bundle: an online store (custom address, inventory synced with in-person), a POS app ($18/yr plus processing), and market pickup windows. Store fee is 2.5%/order or $129/mo. — [Marketspread](https://marketspread.com/online-sales-for-small-vendors)
- BoothBrain: POS + inventory + event prep + payment-method reports, no website. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/boothbrain/id6754667277)
- Before The Booth: show calendar/applications + per-festival ROI, no POS, no website. — [Before The Booth](https://www.beforethebooth.com/)
- MyEventPrep explicitly says it is not a POS. — [MyEventPrep](https://myeventprep.app/)
- Craftybase/Stocksmith: inventory + channel sync including Square in-person, no website and no per-market record. — [Craftybase](https://app.craftybase.com/craft-inventory-software); [Stocksmith pricing](https://stocksmith.io/pricing/)

### Inferences
- A maker today who wants a website, a booth POS and a per-show profit record stitches together something like Square/Etsy, BoothBrain or VendStats, and a spreadsheet or BoothTally. The BohdiAI bundle (site + catalog + POS + market ledger + QR self-buy page, flat $15/$25, no cut) is distinct within the maker-specific category.
- The main all-in-one threat is horizontal platforms (Square Online + Square POS, Shopify POS) that are outside this note's maker-specific scope. Another researcher should cover those.

### Gaps
- Did not check whether Etsy's in-person selling (Square integration) or Shopify POS plus apps add per-event reporting. That is out of scope here.

## Spreadsheet / Notion / printable templates on Etsy: how common, what price

### Takeaway
Craft-fair tracker templates are a well-stocked Etsy category, with multiple dedicated search pages ("craft fair tracker," "craft show spreadsheet," "craft sales tracker," "craft fair inventory spreadsheet") and listings at about $3-$23. Many free printables also exist. This shows a steady demand for per-show profit tracking among makers who do not want or cannot find an app.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy has dedicated market pages for "craft fair tracker," "craft show spreadsheet" (with at least 3 pages of results), "craft sales tracker," "excel craft fair spreadsheet," "craft fair inventory spreadsheet" and "inventory spreadsheet craft show." — [Etsy craft fair tracker](https://www.etsy.com/market/craft_fair_tracker); [Etsy craft show spreadsheet p3](https://www.etsy.com/market/craft_show_spreadsheet?page=3); [Etsy craft sales tracker](https://www.etsy.com/market/craft_sales_tracker)
- Example listing: "Craft Fair Tracker Spreadsheet, Craft Show Planner, Vendor Sales & Profit Tracker, Inventory Log (Google Sheets)" claims revenue, costs, net profit, profit per hour, and a "3x booth fee rule" check. — [Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4530231022/craft-fair-tracker-spreadsheet-craft)
- Observed prices (search snippets, may be sale prices): $3.00 (Google Sheets profit dashboard), $10.54 (Excel profit tracker), $11.99 (Google Sheets vendor tracker, 40% off $19.99). Range seen was about $3-$23. Typical columns: event, date, location, status, setup time, hours, booth fee, application fee, other costs, revenue, starting/remaining stock, "need to make," average profit per fair, best fair. — [Etsy craft sales tracker](https://www.etsy.com/market/craft_sales_tracker); [Etsy Excel listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4363825243/craft-fair-sales-tracker-excel-google)
- Free alternatives: Made Urban's printable stat-tracking spreadsheets (revenue, profit, products sold, price points); Crafty Staci's binder tracking sheet (application and fees, giveaways, sales, after-show notes); a paid Ko-fi printable with event info, inventory and reflection pages; The Maker's Business Toolkit's free Craft Fair Profitability Tracker (profit and ROI per fair); a DIY Beauty Base printable. — [Made Urban](https://www.madeurban.com/blog/7-stats-to-track-at-craft-shows-to-improve-sales/); [Crafty Staci](https://www.craftystaci.com/blog/2015/09/30/craft-show-tracking-sheet); [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/s/50114754aa); [Maker's Business Toolkit](https://makersbusinesstoolkit.com/cfpt/); [DIY Beauty Base](https://diybeautybase.com/free-printable-craft-fair-tracker/)

### Inferences
- The template fields (booth fee, other costs, hours, notes or reflection, unsold stock, profit per fair, best fair) closely match the BohdiAI per-market record. That confirms the feature set matches how makers already think about shows.
- Template buyers pay once ($3-20) and enter data by hand after the show. The BohdiAI edge is that POS sales fill in the record automatically.

### Gaps
- Could not load Etsy pages directly (403), so no review counts or bestseller numbers for templates. No Notion-specific templates were confirmed.

## Reviews, ratings, user counts, activity status (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
Most vendor-specific apps are brand new and unproven: one to three App Store ratings, "early access" labels, and placeholder stats. The older maker tools (Craftybase/Stocksmith, Artwork Archive) are active and established, but they are not market-focused. Nothing in scope looked abandoned. The bigger risk is that many of the new entrants are side projects that may not last.

### Cited Findings
- BoothBrain: 1 rating (5.0), v1.0 12/29/2025, latest v1.2.5. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/boothbrain/id6754667277)
- VendStats: 3 ratings (5.0), latest v2.0.2 (Jul 7). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vendstats-sales-tracker/id6759027702)
- Vendor Toolkit: "in early access." — [Vendor Toolkit](https://vendortools.app/)
- MyEventPrep: "early access," the founder reads every email, free while growing. — [MyEventPrep](https://myeventprep.app/)
- Before The Booth: the directory counter shows "0K+" festivals and "0" states (placeholder). Organizer tier "Coming Soon." — [Before The Booth](https://www.beforethebooth.com/)
- Craftybase is active and recently rebranded to Stocksmith. — [Stocksmith pricing](https://stocksmith.io/pricing/)
- Capterra still lists Craftybase and Artwork Archive with 2026 comparison pages. — [Capterra Craftybase](https://www.capterra.com/compare/138689-144117/Booqable-vs-Craftybase); [Capterra Artwork Archive](https://www.capterra.com/p/169087/Artwork-Archive/)
- Market context from vendors: one Etsy-forum seller pays up to $500/day for a holiday show that fits their audience; another spent over $500 in fees and earned about half back over a season. Guides say patterns show up within 3-4 events. This supports a "was this show worth it" record. — [The Craft Map apps roundup (snippet)](https://www.thecraftmap.com/blog/best-craft-fair-apps-and-tools-for-vendors); [Etsy community](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Etsy-Success/Where-are-craft-fairs-still-profitable-to-participate-in/m-p/27048314/highlight/true)

### Inferences
- With several solo developers shipping vendor trackers within months of each other, it looks easy to build a standalone tracker but hard to win distribution. The bundle with a website (which makers already pay for) is a stronger reason to buy than a tracker alone.

### Gaps
- No Google Play data, no download counts, no revenue figures for any vendor app.
- Sentiment from r/craftfairs, r/Etsy and r/EtsySellers was not retrieved. Search did not surface Reddit threads.
