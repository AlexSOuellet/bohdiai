# Market Plan (Maker Lite becomes the craft-show plan)

Working notes from Alex + Claude, 2026-10-11. Being shaped in conversation; nothing built yet.

## The idea

Maker Lite becomes the plan for makers who like selling locally, at craft shows and markets, and want to bump up those sales. It's a working site plus the full market app, with **no online selling of any kind**. For many makers it's a real alternative to a full shop site.

## In

- A real, working site.
- Their full catalog, **one photo per piece** (Alex: the one photo stays).
- Their markets / where they'll be next, contact, social.
- The **market app**, beefed up (see below).
- Testimonials only as a few hand-entered ones.

## Out

- Online selling — no ordering of any kind, including **reserve for pickup** (Alex: nobody reserves and waits for the next market).
- Promotions.
- Testimonials beyond a few hand-entered.
- Collections.
- Order tracking. The only tracking is **market tracking**.

## Beefing up the market app — under discussion

- **Market-day reminders from buyers:** buyers who pay through the market QR already give a first name and optional email. Add a "Tell me where I'll be next" tick at that step; those buyers get a short note before each market. (Alex: no info-gathering forms in a crowded booth; capturing it at purchase works because buying already collects it.)
- Other candidates raised, not decided: custom order requests with a deposit, a show application tracker, a "what to bring" suggestion from past market results.

## Context

- Seen at a large show (2026-10-10): a seller using a ticker adding machine plus a Square reader, entering every sale twice, long lines, people walking off — the "markets aren't worth it" seller. Not a BohdiAI customer, but the picture of what the market app fixes.
- The maker checkup should eventually be able to recommend this plan to local sellers who want more from markets.
